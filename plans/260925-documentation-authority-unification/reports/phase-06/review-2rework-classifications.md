## Observations

- The standard I applied to headings is the one the checker implies: a heading unit is a structural frame only when its whole shown payload is structure, routing or bookkeeping (migration ledgers, promotion notes, metadata fences, related-file tables). H1 units that wrap a whole verbatim retired document (documentation-governance, documentation-standardization-plan), a status ledger (claim preservation, proof preservation, proposal status, implementation alignment, subcomponent map) or a design proposal (semantic CLI surface) fail it, because the payload carries claims.
- Receipts accepted as bookkeeping or navigation were checked against their text for a hidden status column or rule; the Evidence Sets table in verification/README.md and the related-file tables carry only labels and links.
- Low: the receipt for source-inventory.md unheaded-block-9 also closes the unit that the reviewed retired-unit supersede (ledger/retired-unit-decisions.json, outside the decision directory) names as its target; both statements are true, and the receipt is what keeps the reverse check closed because the checker does not read the retired-unit file.
- Accepting a receipt closes only its bound unit; the executor binds accepted references only after this report is committed. Reworked receipts must be re-issued with a new digest-bound report; the gate rejects any change of class, text or note.
