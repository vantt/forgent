No stratum was short. Blocks are drawn without repeats across strata, ranked by SHA256(seed:path#anchor); script work-blocks/draw.mjs, output work-blocks/draw.json.
- Check per block: every claim is in a source row (decision claim, or text verbatim in the legacy source it cites), or is labelled (`Added in candidate`, candidate header, Source Metadata), or is structural. Source text was tested as a whitespace-normalised substring against the docs tree at merge-base 554401219 and at 5f76eb020 (Phase 6 plan commit, before any carriage). Decision rows were read for the named blocks.
