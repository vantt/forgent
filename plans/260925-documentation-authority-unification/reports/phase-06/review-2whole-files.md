## Verdict

**Classification NOT accepted.** The mechanics are sound (all 68 inputs and the 6 further inputs are byte-contained in their history carriers, see review-2whole-moves.md) but the classification itself is wrong in both directions.

- Moved files (class i): 48 checked individually (not a sample); ok 27; rework 19; hold 2. 21 files still describe live behaviour, live decisions or live vocabulary, or are owner questions, and were wrongly (or doubtfully) moved to non-authority history.
- Retained files (class ii): 20 checked sentence by sentence; ok 5; rework 15; hold 0. Eleven retained files were cut far beyond the retired part: sections that the section review labels live or mixed were moved to history and no longer have a current owner.
- The author's classification evidence is thin: for most moved files it is only the H1 title line (the regex ^(# |Purpose:) matched titles), which says nothing about the main subject, and the shared rationale is a template that is false for the Team Dispatch V1, ADR-006/007 and vocabulary files.
