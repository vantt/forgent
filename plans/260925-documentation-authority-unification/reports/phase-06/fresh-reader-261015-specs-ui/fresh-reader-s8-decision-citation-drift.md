## Scenario C: auto-fix flagged lines

- Q1 What to read first: The candidate spec's Behaviors and Operations ("No line is edited automatically"), the Actors and Access row "Auto-fix a finding: never — detection only", and Open Gaps. cites: `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#behaviors-operations`, `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#actors-access`, `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#open-gaps`
- Q2 Kind of work: Reversing a stated design property (read-only detection) and adding a write path. That is a contract change, not a small feature. The spec's header says the check "only reads files, never writes", so this turns a read-only check into an editor. The legacy spec is the binding owner. cites: `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#behaviors-operations`, `docs/platform/documentation/switchboard.md#2-area-authority-routes`
- Q3 Contract touched: The "What changes: nothing" and "Side effects: none" statements, and the Actors and Access table. The Finding and exit-code contract, where 1 means findings, would also change if fixed lines no longer fail. The scanned files include `docs/backlog.md`. The switchboard classes it as a generated projection to "Do not edit manually". An auto-fix that edits it would write into a projection and not its source. The documented fix is by hand: add the superseding id on the flagged line. cites: `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#actors-access`, `docs/platform/documentation/switchboard.md#3-root-document-routes`, `docs/platform/governance.md#4-authority-matrix`
- Q4 Risk: High relative to A and B. A tool cannot know whether the superseding decision actually restores correct framing, so appending the id would hide dead framing and not resolve it. The check's purpose is that a reader does not re-derive an outdated conclusion. Writing to generated files, or to `docs/specs/*.md`, which is frozen under the legacy ratchet, needs exceptions. The Authority Matrix says a Decision may establish "provenance and supersession" but must not do "silent rewriting of historical intent". cites: `docs/platform/governance.md#4-authority-matrix`, `docs/platform/migration-authoring-rules.md#2-legacy-roots-ratchet-and-exceptions`, `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#open-gaps`

A second scan must exit 0 after the fix. The spec's rules R1 and R2 and the edge cases must still pass. An explicit human decision accepting the reversal is needed first, since "never auto-fix" is a stated property of the spec. cites: `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#business-rules`, `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#edge-cases-settled`, `docs/platform/decision-citation-drift/spec.md#actors-access`
