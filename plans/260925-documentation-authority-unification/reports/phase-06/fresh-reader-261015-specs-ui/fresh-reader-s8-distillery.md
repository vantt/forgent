## Scenario B: someone hand-edits the Status column of the human-readable porting log

- Q1 What to read first: Business rule R2 and the "Advance a candidate's status" behavior in the candidate spec. Because that spec is a candidate, confirm the rule against the binding owner `docs/specs/distillery.md` (not opened by me). cites: `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#business-rules`, `docs/platform/documentation/switchboard.md#2-area-authority-routes`
- Q2 What kind of work: It is a data-correction or process-violation case, not a documentation change. The spec says that after cutover the log is a reflection of the durable record and is never hand-edited for Status. The right path is to fix the status through the `move` verb. cites: `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#business-rules`, `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#entry-points-triggers`
- Q3 What contract it touches: R2 (the durable record is the source of truth) and R1 (only Status is governed; Score, Local, destination, commit and notes remain freely editable by hand). The log is `docs/distillery/porting-log.md` and the record is `docs/distillery/state/porting/`, both named in the Pointers section; I did not verify them. cites: `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#business-rules`, `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#pointers-implementation`
- Q4 How much risk: Moderate. The log and the record can silently diverge. The scoped spec says nothing about whether the sync detects or overwrites a hand edit, so I cannot confirm what happens next. A "wrong" status may also be a legal-but-unexpected value. Illegal moves are refused in full, so a hand edit would bypass that protection. cites: `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#edge-cases-settled`, `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#behaviors-operations`
- Q5 What proof means done: The durable record and the log agree for that candidate. The correction was made through a legal `move` (or the edit was reverted), and nothing else in the log changed. I could not verify from scope whether an automated check exists. cites: `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#behaviors-operations`
- Q6 What learning is left behind: The history is permanent and append-only, so the correction is itself recorded (R2). If a real case shows a status that the lifecycle cannot reach, the spec says that nuance survives only in the free-form Notes column, which is a known gap worth recording in the owner spec. cites: `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#open-gaps`, `docs/platform/distillery/spec.md#business-rules`
