2. What kind of work is this? A change to a locked platform law, not ordinary implementation. The foundations doc says a law is durable and changing one "requires superseding its decision record; do not silently edit the old rationale away": `docs/platform/platform-foundations.md#2-authority`. 3. What contract does it touch?
   - L3 (truth is committed changeset JSONL, the database is a rebuildable view, all writes go through one CLI door): `docs/platform/platform-foundations.md#l3-luật-store-truth-ở-jsonl-db-là-view`.
   - It is mirrored in the work-state area's RUL1-RUL3 (event log as sole truth, projection derived, event written before projection): `docs/platform/work-state/spec.md#11-business-rules`, and in `docs/platform/work-state/spec.md#1-purpose-and-scope`.

4. How much risk? High. Without that threshold being hit, the change would reverse a verified, named decision. The candidate summary also says databases are rebuildable views "unless a named threshold supersedes this": `docs/platform/platform-foundations.md#3-law-summary`.
5. What proof means done? Evidence that the named threshold is met (multi-agent concurrent write as the main load, which breaks the single-writer premise) and a superseding decision record, plus the six-question test and the design gate: `docs/platform/platform-foundations.md#4-design-gate` and `docs/platform/platform-foundations.md#l5-definition-of-done-sáu-câu-hỏi`. Candidate docs do not name a test command.
6. What learning gets left behind? A superseding decision (not an in-place edit): `docs/platform/platform-foundations.md#2-authority`; update the owner doc once and the candidate summary after, per `docs/platform/documentation/switchboard.md#1-operating-invariants`; promote spec-level facts to the area spec per `docs/platform/governance.md#7-discussion-scratchpad-workflow` (promotion targets).
