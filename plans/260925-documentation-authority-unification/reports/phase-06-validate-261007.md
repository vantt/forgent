## 3. Verdict on the reviewer-independence assumption

The assumption is right and necessary but not yet operable. Right: the vocabulary reserves `reviewed` for independent review, and the cost of a self-reviewed shard is a silent content loss (the Phase 5 audit found exactly that kind of drop). Not operable as written: no brief, no read-only procedure, no flip procedure, no author field, no gate check, and every step's done-test needs `reviewed` rows, so the phase stalls until the owner starts a reviewer session per batch (V2). A second session of the same model shares blind spots; the seeded-mutation packs (Steps 2 and 6) are the only measure of that, so they should stay and be answered by the reviewer, not by the executor. The owner cannot review 5,500 rows by hand: owner review should be limited to the lists the plan already routes to the owner (`archive-with-reason`, `delete-as-obsolete`, real conflicts, holds).
