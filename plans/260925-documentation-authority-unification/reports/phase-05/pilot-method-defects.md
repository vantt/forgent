## 3. Verdict

Not PASS (the red-team added M-21 to M-23 and two fixes; it did not change the verdict). Not decidable as PASS-WITH-AMENDMENTS without owner rulings, because three of the stop conditions in the phase file are in play:

1. E1 (control) depends on the reading of "flagged"; the strict reading is a METHOD-MUST-CHANGE trigger.
2. E6 and E7 fail on resolver and consumer-structure grounds that need either a script outside the authorized files (M-11) or a design judgment step (M-13), neither an additive amendment inside this phase.
3. E8 fails for one reader on two reader-derived anchors (M-17).

What the pilot established: the conservation channel works (E2, E3, E4, E5 all met with known-answer tests); the blind source-to-target audit finds real omissions (16 groups registered as `dropped-002` to `dropped-017`) that the old coverage-matrix audit claimed as preserved; and it exposed two defects that matter for Phase 6 regardless of the verdict (M-05 the extractor drops short blocks; M-09 review packs hid block-level anchor errors).

## 4. Cost (E11, recorded, no threshold)

Subagent runs: 41 in process (Sonnet, 112k to 250k tokens each, about 6.5 million tokens in total, run at most 4 in parallel). Wall time of subagent runs, as reported by the harness: the seven Pilot A audit runs 129 to 330 s each (1,759 s for 600 rows: about 2.9 s per row); target-to-source classification 365 s for 191 headings; the discrepancy comparison 856 s; Pilot B candidate authoring 26 to 88 s per document (347 s for 6 documents); ledger authors 54 to 113 s per shard (491 s for 366 rows: 1.3 s per row); independent reviewers 24 to 117 s per shard (332 s for 366 rows: 0.9 s per row); alias test 211 s; link preview 282 s; fresh readers about 55 s each.

Extrapolation to Phase 6 (an estimate, not a measurement of Phase 6): the work scales with rows, not with documents. Author plus reviewer cost about 4.8k tokens and 2.2 s of subagent time per row for verbatim carries (Pilot B, 366 rows); the blind audit about 2.4k tokens and 2.9 s per row (Pilot A, 600 rows). One thousand rows therefore cost about 5 million tokens and about 37 minutes of serial subagent time, about 10 minutes at four in parallel, plus the lead's reverse checks and the deterministic gates (a gate run is about 40 s and 2 GB). Rows needing real judgment (paraphrased targets, Pilot A) are about half as expensive per row but find nothing by text match, so they cannot be shortened by mechanical checks (M-19).
