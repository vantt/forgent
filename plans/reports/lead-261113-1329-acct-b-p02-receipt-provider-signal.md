## Lessons
- The census hook binds every commit touching a scanned test file; plan for a regenerate per commit.
- Return `(reason, signal)` together from the one read so the two can never come from different screens.
- Sub-second pane tests are load-sensitive; confirm a timeout alone before treating it as red.
