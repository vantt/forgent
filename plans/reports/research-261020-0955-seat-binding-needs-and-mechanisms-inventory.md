### 4.2 The decision order as the code runs it

For one seat of one Unit. Steps 1-14 are `bind()` (pure over config); 15-19 are dispatch. File and line references are in 4.1.

0. **Before the run.** `run.mjs:381` dry-binds every seat of the pattern with the run's overrides: a pin no seat takes, or a quarantined pinned account, is refused before the unit run exists. A resumed run reuses the bindings recorded in `unit.json` (`run.mjs:925`).
1. **Seat ask.** The pattern supplies `role`, the role's objective, `readOnly`, `independentOf` (role names turned into the executors that played them, via the role ledger) or `preferIndependentOf` (the ledger's seats so far); the runner adds the run's `overrides` and `blind` (`run.mjs:900-935`).
2. **Matching override.** The first override whose `scope.unit` and `scope.role` match (or that has no scope) is used alone; later ones are ignored (`bind:89-99`). Workflow runs emit seat-scoped persona overrides (`--seats`), then one unscoped `human-cli` fallback carrying effort, timeout and account (`unit-handoff.mjs:177-205`).
3. **Posture.** `read-only` unless the role is `producer` and the unit has `writes`.
4. **Capability config.** Exact key, else the verb-only key, requiring a usable `prefer` first.
5. **Tier.** `effectiveRigor` = stronger of unit rigor and capability rigor (else whichever exists, else standard); `rigorToTier`; if the matching override has `tier`, the stronger of the two (raise only); clamp to the ceiling (`maxTier`, raised to the capability's own rigor tier); the source string records `rigor:<r>`, `override:<origin>` or `clamped:maxTier(...)`.
6. **Candidate pool.** An override `executor` makes a pool of one. Otherwise `unit.prefer` entries, then `capabilities.<cap>.prefer`, deduped (a bare unit executor drops that executor's capability entries). A capability pool is the **same for every role** of the unit.
7. **Expand to candidates.** For each executor: family, mechanism (checker and panel roles out-of-process), launch (invocation and transport by posture and herdr presence; a named invocation stays) and, for a seat that leases an account (out-of-process agent, not a tool), **one candidate per entry of the family's cell at this tier**, each with `pool` and `model`. A missing cell yields one candidate carrying a `tier-cell-missing` problem. A seat that does not lease (in-process, inline, tool) reads the model best-effort or binds `default`.
8. **Filter** (first failing rule wins, in this order): unknown executor (unit prefer only), governance, skills, pool excluded (a dead login earlier in this unit), independence (hard; `acceptDependence` plus `human-cli` waives), posture unavailable, blind-in-process, missing cell, pool unusable (no enabled account with the needed store).
9. **Pick.** First binding: candidates in order, the first that passes; with a spread request, the family used by the fewest earlier seats (0 stops the search), ties by order. After a failure (walk): `walkOrder` = remaining entries of the failed family, then the `tierFallback.<tier>` families (limited to families in this pool) or the rest of the pool; already-tried (executor, invocation, pool, model) removed; spread still ranks.
10. **No candidate.** A producer with a live non-headless lead and no skill need falls back inline; otherwise refuse with the last rejection reason (`no-candidate`, `headless-no-executor`, `independence`, `tier-cell-missing`, ...).
11. **Account pin.** If the matching override has `account` (`pool/account`): re-pick the candidate of the same family whose pool matches; resolve against the inventory; an unknown or disabled account refuses the seat; other pool, no inventory or in-process binds unpinned with the reason recorded.
12. **Persona** (override > unit > locked > capability; a lock conflict refuses).
13. **Reasoning effort** and **timeout** (override, else unit; validated against the executor).
14. **Provenance** per field: value and source; `pool`, `skipped` candidates, `familySpread`, `account`, ceiling.
15. **Policy and gate.** `assignment-runner` derives the assignment policy; the policy raises the tier bind gave it and applies no ceiling; a mutating assignment is re-bound by the write gate through `walkStateOf` (without spread or independence inputs, D16).
16. **Lease.** State key `legacyStateKey(pool)`; accounts in declared order, sticky first, skipping quarantined, disabled, or without a store for the invocation's `credentialStore.use`; if none is usable, the lazy probe may unlock an `auth-token` account (30 min cooldown); a pool with no inventory leases nothing (the executor's own login). The first account takes every lease until it cannot (D19).
17. **Confinement and transport.** The store files named by `credentialStore.use` are copied into a private home bound to `homeEnv`; bwrap, herdr or cli spawn (RUL78, RUL105). A pinned seat narrows the inventory to that account.
18. **On a provider limit** (`run.mjs:1155-1195`): classify (credential: quarantine `auth-token`; quota: quarantine until reset or 1 h); stop if a writing seat changed the worktree, if the seat launch ceiling is spent, or if the seat is pinned (`fallbackExhausted`); else rotate to another account of the same pool while healthy accounts remain and retries < accounts - 1 (`-fb<n>`); else add the pool to `excludePools` when the cause was credential, re-read independence and spread, and walk (step 9, walk branch); with no candidate left: `fallbackExhausted` with the reason.
19. **Retry provenance** (`fallbackFrom.kind` is `account-rotation`, or `next-candidate` with `walk: entry|family`); the role ledger records each executor the seat ran.
