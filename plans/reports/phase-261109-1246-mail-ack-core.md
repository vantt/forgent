## Deadline rules
- Due `sent + reply-by`. Unoffered at due: `ack-not-offered`, watch stays open. Offered (set in `finish`): due restarts at `offered + reply-by`; unacknowledged then: `ack-overdue`.
- `ack-overdue-again` replaces either wording when another retained watch from the same sender to the same recipient already fired. A watch's own first notice does not escalate its second.
- Acked after the deadline still sets `acked`. Finished watches kept one header horizon; open watches whose header is refused or gone are dropped; remote watches (`to_machine`) are skipped by the sweep (P5).
