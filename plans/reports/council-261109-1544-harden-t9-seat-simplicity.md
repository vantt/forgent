## Part A. Target shapes

Plain statement: T9 is not too big because the arms are heavy. It is big because **each arm kept the logic it was born with and the shell kept five jobs that belong lower** (hook decisions, Event Store lifecycle and store guards, verb property sets, root and host-binary lookup, run flag folding), and because three Rust crates each copied one 15-line command builder. After the cut every arm is parse, call one use case, print; Work arms are untouched.

## Positions on the baseline's five open questions

**U1. Who owns `knowledge`, `doc`, `doc-registry`, `docs-index`, `doc-sources`, `authoritative-match`, `decision`, `decision-index`, `compound`, `context-render`?** Treat them as Work for T9 (Part C): leave every one alone and hand the cluster to the Work council as its own sub-question.
