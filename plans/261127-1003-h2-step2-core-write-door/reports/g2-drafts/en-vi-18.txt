When fgOS is set up in a project it scans read-only for other agent harnesses (.bee, .claude, .codex, .cursor directories, plus a bee block in AGENTS.md) and writes `.fgos/coexistence.json` declaring its own territory: the .fgos data directory, worktrees under the temp dir, and fgw/* branches. A detection problem, such as an unreadable AGENTS.md, is recorded in the manifest and never fails init.
