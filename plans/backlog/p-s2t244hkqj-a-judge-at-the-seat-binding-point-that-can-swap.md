# Why

Until it exists the supervisor and lead personas are pinned to Claude Sonnet in project config (see p-of5vpvce3e): a fixed pin is safe but cannot use another family when Claude capacity runs short. The owner wants a judge, at the point where a seat is bound to an account, that decides whether another family is good enough for the role and swaps to it when it is. Not yet shaped: what the judge measures (a quality bar per role, from evidence such as the stalls of Gemini-run leads), where it sits in the existing seat-binding door, and whether it reuses the capacity selection already there. Open as a discussion first; do not build before the owner picks it.

# Done when

A shaped discussion states the quality bar per head role, the judge's inputs and the door it joins, with no second selection mechanism beside the capacity selection.
