# Why

Miu needs a defined reach to coordinate any session or item (read the portfolio, write backlog and questions, start and stop shapers and planners, queue or delegate work, message peers) plus a bounded fast route for urgent small chores; the supervisor needs the cyclic main-work reach. Today both are blocked by sandbox and write-door gaps (see the secretary capabilities lane).

# Done when

A written split of reach between Miu and the supervisor, with the urgent-chore route and its limits, agreed by the owner; each power is granted through a named door and proven live from the sandbox.
