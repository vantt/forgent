# Why

Today an urgent matter can be pushed onto a lead that is mid-plan, which splits that lead's attention and its footprint. The owner's rule: the supervisor does not give urgent work to a busy lead; to take a lane it asks that lead to pause and yield the lane, then starts a different lead on the urgent item. The paused lead resumes when the lane is free again. Decided by the owner 2026-11-21: an item is urgent when the owner says so, or when an agent finds that it must be done first so that other work goes better. Not yet shaped: how a lead pauses safely mid-phase (its worktree and unlanded work) and how the yield and resume are recorded. Related: p-waxzxz5mio (idle lead), p-xnu3jnds2u (powers of the supervisor).

# Done when

The supervisor never assigns an urgent item to a busy lead; taking a lane goes through a recorded pause-and-yield request to that lead, then a separate lead starts the urgent item; an owning test covers a busy lane and an urgent item.
