# Why

`fgos questions cancel` accepts only the asker, a session of the asker's head role, or a head for a migrated question. On 2026-11-22 five open questions were moot or already decided: `q-4qtu54bnxz` and `q-vgwalbohwj` (plan `261122-0800` done), and `q-r5w6funyv5`, `q-qki26cwunf`, `q-d5mrno2mfk` (heads-stable footprint widenings the supervisor decided inside the approved frame). Their askers are lead heads of plans that have ended or moved on. The secretary and the supervisor were both refused `not-asker`. The only other door, `questions answer`, records a choice as the owner's, so closing a moot question that way writes a false owner decision. The owner kept seeing these as open and as waiting on them.

# Done when

One door closes a question that is moot or decided elsewhere, whoever asked it, records who closed it and why, and never records an owner answer. Closing is allowed to the supervisor and the secretary. A question whose `ref` plan is done is closed or flagged without the asker. One owning test drives a question whose asker session has ended.
