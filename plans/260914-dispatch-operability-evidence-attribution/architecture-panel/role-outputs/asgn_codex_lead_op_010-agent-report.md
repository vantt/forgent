architecture and the DOEA-01..DOEA-13 direction, but do not let the present
`READY` labels authorize promotion or a new implementation track until the

edits” before those prerequisites land would let the conclusion outrun its own
gate.

## Codex-only limitation

Every static actor in the supplemental panel used the Codex executor. The run
therefore has role separation, but **no provider-family diversity**. Shared
model priors and correlated blind spots remain plausible. Role names and
separate assignment IDs do not establish distinct principals or independent
cross-provider corroboration, and the final record must not imply otherwise.

This limitation does not by itself falsify the architecture. It caps confidence
and leaves a governance choice: if D06 strictly requires independent review,
obtain review from a genuinely independent principal/provider; otherwise the
authorized owner must durably amend or waive that acceptance criterion while
retaining the “Codex-only, non-cross-provider” disclosure.

- The constraint advocate warns that terminal-authority migration, CAS races,

  Those warnings survive; paper readiness must never be presented as runtime
  proof.

## What remains for implementation, not design

After the documentation/evidence gate restores `READY`, a separate
implementation track must build and prove the runtime behavior: the v2 worker
claim/effective-contract path; deterministic RunResult v2 and legacy-v1
normalization; portable read-only inspection; CAS-guarded reconciliation;
compatibility and corrupt/ambiguous-state handling; and selected-adapter,
persistence-path production drills, including concurrency and all forbidden
recovery routes.

Those drills decide whether the design survives contact with the real runtime.
They are implementation exit criteria, not reasons to invent more paper design
now. A failed drill reopens its owning DOEA decision; until the drills pass,
none of `dispatch.runtime.inspect`, `dispatch.runtime.reconcile`, RunResult v2,
or effective execution-contract behavior may be described as shipped or safe
to enable.

## Final recommendation

Record D06 as **`NOT READY — documentation/evidence repair required`**. Keep the
architecture, perform the six repair groups above, rerun/relink the affected
panel checks, and restore exact `READY` only when no HIGH remains and every
MEDIUM finding has a durable disposition and recheck. Then hand the unchanged
architectural direction to a separate implementation track.
