# Phase 05 - Comparative Proof And Promotion

Depends on: Phase 04 closed.

## Objective

Prove that productization added safety and durability without making the panel
less intelligent. Compare the manual playbook and production skill on real
architecture cases, close regressions, and promote only evidence-backed
behavior.

## Cells

### P05.1 - Hard conformance and recovery proof

Exercise the installed product path against immutable fixtures for every
authority, visibility, aggregation, routing, bound, and replay claim. Kill the
driver after meaningful durable progress and resume from a fresh process with
only the installed skill plus session id. Store requests, events, replay,
RunResults, routing provenance, attacks, and outcomes under `proofs/P05.1/`.

### P05.2 - Comparative live proof and promotion

Run real clear and unclear cases with the Phase 01 rubric and a person's
Decision Dialogue. Compare manual and product behavior row by row. Route soul
regressions to P04 and hard gaps to P03 only with evidence. After independent
approval, promote contracts/doctrine/docs, update proposal status and changelog,
run final checks, and close the track.

## Requirements

- Run the production panel on clear and unclear real-project questions
  comparable to Phase 01's cases, including a real person in Decision Dialogue.
- Evaluate both paths with the same qualitative rubric: improved understanding,
  necessity and quality of questions, credibility of alternatives, use of
  disconfirming evidence, willingness to revise, dissent preservation,
  recommendation calibration, explanation quality, and decision usefulness.
- Record where the hard shell improves safety/recovery and where it introduces
  stiffness, ceremony, context loss, or degraded prose.
- Treat a material qualitative regression as a failed proof even when all
  schema and conformance tests pass. Fix soul/prose in Phase 04; change the hard
  shell only when evidence proves it is the source of the regression.
- Exercise controlled reveal, specialist pull-in, aggregation, recheck,
  selective reopen, crash/fresh-process resume, human-decision provenance,
  bounds, and Work isolation through the real product path.
- Exercise heterogeneous dispatch in one real panel: at least two distinct
  safe executor/provider bindings, explicit role-level tiers, fresh independent role
  contexts, and RunResult provenance matching every selected binding.
- Re-run the advisory shape with the available homogeneous fallback and report
  the qualitative difference. Provider diversity is a controllable capability,
  not an unmeasured claim of automatic superiority.
- Independently review and red-team the final implementation. The red-team must
  attack both legal bypasses and bad-advisor failure modes.
- Promote settled hard behavior into canonical Coordination contracts and soft
  behavior into the appropriate domain doctrine/skill documentation. Update
  reading maps, Group Thinking docs, examples, changelog,
  `docs/architect/agent-coordination/verification/README.md`, and the track
  verification index without presenting prose judgment as a kernel guarantee.

## Live Proof Boundary

Use real projects outside `forgentX`. Record repository instructions,
configured executors, exact fgOS version, baseline state, evidence sources, and
the person's evaluation. A scripted fake-human transcript cannot prove the
Decision Dialogue. The panel must not mutate code, git, or Work state.

## Final Verification

Run the focused coordination/verbs/CLI/architecture suites, skill projection
checks, all protocol conformance cases, relative-link checks, and full
`npm test`. Use `detect_changes()` against `main` before final commit.

The P05.2 report names commit hashes, binary/version, external project roots,
requested actor bindings, actual RunResult executor/provider/model/tier, human
interactions, dispositions, baseline/final test counts, remaining limitations,
and the manual-vs-product qualitative matrix. Narration alone proves nothing.

## Exit

The shipped panel is measurably at least as useful as its manual prompt, more
auditable and resumable, and no less able to understand, reframe, disagree,
explain, and advise. Remaining limitations are named honestly as either hard
capability deferrals or soft-intelligence improvements.
