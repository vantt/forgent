check (`undefined !== "real-driver"` also happens to throw) — it fails
closed one full validation layer upstream, structurally, before the resume
boundary is reached at all.

Independently constructed three variants against an already-open session:
`writerId` key entirely absent, `writerId: null`, `writerId: ""`. All
three refused (non-zero exit); the missing-key case's stderr explicitly
names `"writerId" is required`. Zero side effects on the manifest across
all three attempts.
