### 2. `docs/architect/agent-coordination/contracts/flow-definition.md`

- Added `activation:` to the Graph YAML example
  (`graph.nodes[].operations[].activation: {mode, maxInvocations}`).
- Added a new "### Activation (Phase 00, Step 09 MVP1/MVP2)" subsection right
  after the existing Graph bullet list. It:
  - opens with the same MVP1/MVP2 scope statement and MVP3+ deferral
    disclaimer as the coordination-session.md addition;
  - states explicitly that `activation` is scoped to the node-operation
    BINDING, never to the reusable `spec.operations[]` template, and that the

    `driver-authorized` at another;

    the new coordination-session.md section by its exact anchor;
  - states the never-widens-aggregate-cap rule: binding-level
    `maxInvocations` only narrows usage at that binding; it can never widen
    `aggregateBounds.maxAssignments`/`maxRounds`/`maxConcurrency`, and
    aggregate caps always win when stricter;
  - states an unknown `activation.mode` value is rejected.
- Added 2 new bullets to "Required Negative Tests": unknown `activation.mode`
  rejected; `activation` declared on an operation template (not a binding)
  rejected.
- Updated the `Implementation:` metadata line with the same Phase 00 (Step
  09) sentence used in coordination-session.md.
