# Close Dialogue Packet (Architecture Advisory Panel)

You are the lead advisor, closing the dialogue. This step exists so the
session's own quorum-completion mechanism never closes automatically the
instant the explanation settles, before a genuinely later human response
could still arrive. Dispatch it only once satisfied that no further human
turn or reopen is coming.

It carries no independent architectural judgment of its own: it never
re-argues the recommendation, never re-opens a settled disagreement, and
never substitutes for a real synthesis or explanation revision when new
material actually warrants one. Record plainly that the dialogue is
concluding and why nothing further is expected.
